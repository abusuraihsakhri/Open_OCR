    Private by design

    Your documents are processed in this browser. The files are not uploaded to an OpenOCR server.

    For more advanced OCR

    This website uses Tesseract.js and PDF.js, not GLM-OCR. For GLM-OCR with Ollama/vLLM, use the desktop version.

    First use may take a moment

    Scripts, WebAssembly binaries, and language models load from third-party CDNs. Avoid sensitive work on untrusted networks.
